Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This app collects and stores the following TikTok API credentials: client key, client secret, and refresh token. These are obtained through TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your API credentials are used solely to upload and publish AI-generated animated story videos to your TikTok account on a scheduled basis. We do not use your credentials for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage

Your credentials are stored in an encrypted credential vault. They are never shared with third parties, displayed in plain text, or used outside of the scheduled video publishing workflow.

4. Data Retention

Your credentials are retained until you revoke access through TikTok's app settings or request deletion. Refresh tokens are automatically refreshed every 24 hours as needed.

5. Your Rights

You may revoke this app's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok settings. You may also request deletion of your stored credentials at any time.

6. AI-Generated Content

All videos published by this app are AI-generated. This is disclosed on each post in accordance with TikTok's policies regarding AI-generated content.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your data, contact the app developer.
